# Brand assets

The files in this folder describe and draw the blipbox identity. The
MIT license at the root of this repository covers the code, including the
SVG markup and the CSS, but it does not grant rights to the blipbox name,
the wordmark, or the cube mark. Those belong to Nimbous.

You may:

- use the name and the mark to refer to blipbox and its projects, for
  example in an article, a talk, a package listing, or a link;
- reproduce the mark unmodified at the sizes and clear space described in
  BRAND.md when you do so.

You may not:

- use the name or the mark as your own brand or product name, or as part
  of one;
- use them in a way that suggests blipbox or Nimbous endorses you;
- modify the mark, recolor it, or combine it with other marks.

The project sprites generated by `assets/main.js` for individual boxes are
part of those projects and follow their licenses.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/blip-box/blipbox-www.
